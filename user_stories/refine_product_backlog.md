# User Stories - Refine Your Product Backlog

## Review Existing Backlog Items
**Title:**  
_As a product manager, I want to review existing backlog items regularly, so that the product roadmap stays relevant._

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Backlog items are listed with their current status and priority.
2. Option to archive or update outdated items.

**Priority:** Medium  
**Story Points:** 3

---

## Prioritize Backlog Items
**Title:**  
_As a product manager, I want to prioritize backlog items based on business goals and user needs, so that the most valuable features are developed first._

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Backlog items can be ranked by drag-and-drop or a priority field.
2. Top-priority items are visually highlighted.

**Priority:** High  
**Story Points:** 3

---

## Break Down Epics into Smaller Tasks
**Title:**  
_As a product manager, I want to break down epics into smaller, actionable tasks, so that they are easier to plan and execute._

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Epics can be divided into multiple tasks.
2. Each task inherits relevant metadata (e.g., labels, assignee) from the epic.
3. Parent-child relationships are maintained in the backlog.

**Priority:** High  
**Story Points:** 4

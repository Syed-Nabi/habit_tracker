# User Stories - Triage New Issues

## Review Incoming Issues
**Title:**  
_As a product manager, I want to review new issues reported by users or team members, so that I can determine their priority and validity._

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. All new issues are listed in a "New Issues" section.
2. Issues display relevant details such as title, description, and reporter.
3. Option to mark issues as valid, invalid, or duplicate.

**Priority:** High  
**Story Points:** 3

---

## Assign Issues to Team Members
**Title:**  
_As a product manager, I want to assign validated issues to team members, so that they can be resolved efficiently._

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Validated issues can be assigned to team members.
2. Assignment sends notifications to the assignee.
3. Assignee and status are updated in the issue tracker.

**Priority:** Medium  
**Story Points:** 2

---

## Categorize Issues by Severity
**Title:**  
_As a product manager, I want to categorize issues by severity, so that the most critical ones are addressed first._

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Categories include "Low," "Medium," "High," and "Critical."
2. Issues can be re-categorized based on updates or re-evaluation.

**Priority:** High  
**Story Points:** 3
